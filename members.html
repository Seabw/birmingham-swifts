Connecting Beyond the Run

Our community thrives on and off the towpaths. Once you join a run, you'll be invited to our official club WhatsApp community featuring dedicated sub-channels for Thursday social runs, weekend parkrun carpooling, marathon training groups, and social night coordination.

You can also join over 150+ Swifts on our Strava Club page, where members give each other kudos, track weekly mileage, and celebrate training breakthroughs!
