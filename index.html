WHO WE ARE

A Welcoming Community for Every Runner

Established in Birmingham to create a safe, supportive, and social space where LGBTQ+ individuals and allies can discover the joy of running together.

🏳️‍🌈

100% Inclusive Environment

Regardless of your sexual orientation, gender identity, background, or running pace, you will always find a warm welcome and genuine camaraderie.

🛡️

No Runner Left Behind

Every group run features designated Run Leaders and friendly Tail Walkers/Tail Runners. We run at a conversational pace and regather at checkpoints.

🍻

Running & Social Balance

Running is only half the fun! We host post-run drinks in Birmingham's Gay Village, weekend brunch runs, pride parade squads, and regular social nights.

Find Your Next Run

We organize 3 regular weekly sessions across Birmingham catering to all distances and paces.

Thursday Evening • Main Run ⏰ 6:50 PM Meet (7:00 PM Start)

Main Club Social Run (5k / 7k / 10k & Walk-to-Run)

📍 Pagoda Island, Bath Row / Gay Village, Birmingham

Our official flagship weekly club run along Birmingham's canals and scenic streets. Features 5 distinct pace groups plus a dedicated Starting Out / Walk-to-Run group. Bag drop and toilets available, followed by social drinks at The Fox!

Walk-to-Run 7:00-8:00 /km 6:00-7:00 /km 5:00-6:00 /km < 5:00 /km
Saturday Morning • Unofficial ⏰ 8:45 AM Meet (9:00 AM Start)

Swifts Parkrun Tour & Social Coffee

📍 Cannon Hill, Edgbaston, Perry Hall, Babbs Mill & Woodgate

Unofficial weekly club meet-ups at local Birmingham 5k parkruns. Swifts wear club vests, run or walk at their own pace, and regather afterwards for coffee, pastries, and social catch-ups.

Sunday Long Run (Half, Marathon & Ultra Training)

Unofficial long distance runs organized by club members preparing for half marathons, full marathons, or ultra-distance trail races (10k to 30k+). Great camaraderie, steady conversational pacing, and regather checkpoints.

CLUB INITIATIVES

Special Programs & Challenges

From seasonal challenges to beginner graduations, see what makes Birmingham Swifts unique.

🌟 ANNUAL FLAGSHIP

Swiftember Challenge 2026

Our massive annual September challenge where runners pledge a monthly mileage target (25km to 250km+), log Strava activities, earn awards like Goal Setter, Rising Swift, and Road Warrior, and cheer on the whole club.

🐣 BEGINNERS

Couch to 5k (C25K) Program

A dedicated, supportive 9-week course designed for complete beginners. Led by certified run leaders in a safe, judgment-free environment, culminating in a festive 5k graduation run!

🏳️‍🌈 SOCIAL & EVENTS

Pride & Social Calendar

We proudly march in Birmingham Pride, organize cheer squads for the Great Birmingham Run and Birmingham Half Marathon, and host regular social nights, quizzes, and weekend away trips.

Club Membership & Affiliation

All club runs are open to everyone with no fees. You can also become an official member to support the club or register with England Athletics.

Club Runner & Tasters

£0 / open to all
  • Join Thursday social runs & weekend meetups
  • All 5+ pace groups and Walk-to-Run
  • Friendly run leaders & tail walkers
  • Post-run social drinks at The Fox

Club Supporter Member

£15 / year
  • Official Birmingham Swifts membership
  • Club kit discounts (pride vests, tees & hoodies)
  • Swiftember challenge entry & annual awards
  • Private club WhatsApp & member community
  • Supports club equipment, venues & run leader training

England Athletics Affiliated

£35 / year
  • All Club Supporter benefits included
  • Official England Athletics (EA) athlete registration
  • £2+ discount on all UK road & trail races
  • London Marathon club ballot entry eligibility
  • Personal race insurance & EA partner perks
