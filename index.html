CLUB COMMITTEE

Swifts Committee

The Birmingham Swifts are steered by a passionate, democratically elected committee of volunteers. From club governance, EA affiliations, race entries, and welfare safeguarding to finances and vibrant social events, our committee dedicates countless hours each week to keep our community safe, inclusive, well-organized, and thriving for everyone.

Meet The Committee
COACHES & LEADERS

Coaches & Run Leaders

Our England Athletics (EA) qualified coaches design structured interval training, bespoke marathon plans, and safe technique progressions. Working alongside them, our dedicated team of 15 volunteer Run Leaders guide our flagship Thursday evening social runs, ensuring safe canal and street routes, welcoming runners of all abilities, and our golden rule: no runner is ever left behind.

If you would like to support the club and get kit discounts, you can choose our optional £15/year club membership, or £35/year England Athletics affiliation for official racing licences and marathon ballot access.

We currently hold three weekly sessions: our flagship Thursday Social Run (bag drop 6:30 PM at The Fox in the Gay Village, 6:50 PM meet at Pagoda Island); Saturday Parkruns (unofficial club meetups at local Birmingham 5k events followed by coffee); and Sunday Long Runs (unofficial runs for members training for half marathons, full marathons, or ultras).

Everyone is welcome! We are an LGBTQ+ and Allies running club. Whether you are gay, lesbian, bisexual, trans, non-binary, queer, or a supportive ally, you will find a warm, friendly community.
