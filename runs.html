Weekly Runs & Sessions

We organize three regular weekly sessions across Birmingham. Our official Thursday social run is guided by trained Run Leaders and tail walkers, with informal weekend community meetups.

Thursday Evening • Main Run ⏰ 6:50 PM Meet (7:00 PM Start)

Main Club Social Run (5k / 7k / 10k & Walk-to-Run)

📍 Pagoda Island, Bath Row / Gay Village, Birmingham

Our official flagship weekly club session along Birmingham's canal towpaths and illuminated street routes. Welcomes runners and walkers of all abilities with a dedicated Starting Out / Walk-to-Run group. Bag drop and restrooms available before departure, followed by social drinks at The Fox!

Walk-to-Run 5k & 7k Routes All Abilities Welcome Tail Walkers
Saturday Morning • Unofficial ⏰ 8:45 AM Meet (9:00 AM Start)

Swifts Parkrun Tour & Social Coffee

📍 Cannon Hill, Edgbaston Reservoir, Perry Hall, Babbs Mill & Woodgate Valley

Weekly informal club meet-ups at local Birmingham 5k parkrun events. Swifts wear their club vests, run or walk at their own preferred pace, and regather afterwards for coffee, breakfast pastries, and weekend catch-ups.

5k Free Community Run Local Parkruns Across Birmingham All Paces Welcome Post-Run Coffee & Chat
Sunday Morning • Unofficial ⏰ 9:00 AM / 9:30 AM Meet

Sunday Long Run (Half, Marathon & Ultra Training)

📍 Sutton Park, Lickey Hills, Canal Towpaths & Off-Road Trails

Unofficial endurance runs organized by members training for autumn and spring half marathons, full marathons (London, Manchester, Chicago, Berlin), or ultra-distance trail races (10k to 30k+). Steady conversational pacing with frequent regather checkpoints.

10k – 30k+ Distance Half & Full Marathon Prep Ultra Distance Trails Regroup Checkpoints

What to Expect at Your First Thursday Run

Arrival & Briefing: We gather from 6:50 PM at Pagoda Island on Bath Row. Look out for the bright Swifts running vests and friendly run leaders. At 7:00 PM sharp, we do announcements and group introductions before heading off.

Bag Drop & Facilities: You can securely leave coats and bags at our meet partner venue in the Gay Village before we start, and restrooms are available.

What to Wear: Comfortable trainers and running kit. During autumn and winter months, high-visibility clothing and a small running headtorch or chest light are strongly recommended.

The Social: After each run, everyone is invited to join us for a drink (alcoholic or soft) and a chat at The Fox. It's the best way to get to know your fellow runners!
