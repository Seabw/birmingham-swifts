Membership FAQs

Do I have to pay to run with Birmingham Swifts?
No! All of our weekly Thursday sessions and weekend runs are completely free and open to everyone. You do not need to be a paid member to join our group runs.

What is England Athletics (EA) affiliation?
England Athletics is the national governing body for running clubs in England. Becoming an affiliated member gives you a registered athlete license number, personal accident insurance during races and training, discounted entry fees on UK road and trail races, and access to our annual club London Marathon ballot allocation.

Can I transfer my EA affiliation from another club?
Yes! If you are already affiliated with another running club and would like Birmingham Swifts to become your first-claim or second-claim club, you can request an easy online transfer through the England Athletics myAthletics portal.

Where does the £15 / £35 membership fee go?
The club is entirely run by volunteers and non-profit. The £15 supporter fee directly covers club equipment (high-vis vests, first aid supplies, bag drop venue hire, website maintenance) and subsidizes training courses for new Run Leaders. In the £35 tier, £19 goes directly to England Athletics for your national licence fee.
