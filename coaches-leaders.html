Coaches & Run Leaders

Meet our qualified England Athletics coaching staff and our enthusiastic team of volunteer Run Leaders who guide our weekly Thursday social runs and keep every session safe and welcoming.

Certified Coaching & Dedicated Run Leadership

At Birmingham Swifts, safety, encouragement, and inclusive athletic development go hand in hand. Every weekly run is supported by certified volunteers who give their time freely so you can enjoy your running journey without stress or pressure:

England Athletics Qualified Coaches (3 Coaches): Our coaches hold certified England Athletics qualifications (Coach in Running Fitness / Coaching Assistant). They design progressive training cycles, coach safe running biomechanics, and provide tailored marathon and endurance guidance.

Volunteer Run Leaders (15 Leaders): Certified through England Athletics' Leadership in Running Fitness (LiRF) program. They plan and guide our official Thursday evening social routes (5k, 7k, 10k) along Birmingham's canal towpaths and city streets, and act as dedicated tail runners ensuring no runner is ever left behind. (Note: Saturday Parkruns and Sunday Long Runs are informal, member-led community meetups without designated leaders).

STRUCTURED TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT

EA Qualified Coaches

Guiding your fitness with professional England Athletics accreditation, sports science principles, and genuine encouragement.

EA Coach in Running Fitness (CiRF)

Christopher Bainbridge

Head Coach • Endurance & Marathon Lead

Christopher has been coaching with Swifts for over 7 years. A veteran of 18 marathons and numerous ultras, he creates our structured autumn and spring marathon training plans. He focuses on sustainable aerobic conditioning, race-day nutrition strategies, and keeping running joyful.

"Consistency beats intensity every single time. My goal is to help every Swift cross their finish line smiling and injury-free."
EA Coaching Assistant & CiRF

Mark Blakeman

Foundation & Couch to 5k Lead Coach

Mark leads our beginner development and Couch to 5k programs. With a background in sports physiotherapy, Mark specializes in safe biomechanics, gradual impact progression, and helping nervous first-timers discover that they belong in the running world.

"You are a runner the very moment you put on your trainers. Speed doesn't matter — having fun and moving together does."
EA Athletics Coach

Alice Wilkinson

Track Intervals & Speedwork Coach

Alice oversees our seasonal track speed sessions and tempo workouts. She works closely with runners aiming for 5k, 10k, and half marathon personal bests, integrating cadence drills, stride mechanics, and tactical pacing into accessible, high-energy sessions.

"Intervals aren't about punishing yourself; they're about teaching your mind and legs to be confident when the pace picks up."
